How the scores work, and what is approximated

Screen 1. Quality gate: ROE ≥ 12%, operating margin ≥ 10%, debt-to-equity ≤ 200%. Missing gate data fails the gate. Value score: FCF yield (25), PEG (20), forward earnings yield (15), discount from 52-week high (20), upside to mean target (10), price vs lowest target (10), each a straight-line ramp between its zero and full points; missing factors are left out and the rest rescaled. Bonus up to 5 for ROE above 15% (full at 30%) and up to 5 for operating margin above 20% (full at 40%); capped at 100.

Screen 2. Each checklist lens scores the share of its checks passed; checks that can't be computed are skipped. Greenblatt is the Magic Formula percentile (banks, insurers and other financials, and utilities excluded). Piotroski is F-score ÷ 9. Margin of safety: 10-year DCF on 3-year average FCF, growth = lower of sales growth, EPS growth or 12%, fading to 3%, 10% discount rate, 2.5% terminal growth, plus cash minus debt; financials use justified P/B. 50%+ discount scores 100. MASTERS score = weighted average with each lens pulled 15% toward 50. Consensus = lenses scoring 70+.

Data. Yahoo Finance via yfinance, 4 annual reports per company, so "every year" means the last four. Approximations and choices the written rules leave open are marked ASSUMPTION in scoring.py.

Past drops. For each stock on the ADD-REVIEW list or in the top 10: its own daily adjusted price history (as far back as Yahoo has it), every earlier time it fell at least as far below its trailing 52-week high as today (10% minimum). Each episode ends when the price is back at that prior high; the next can only start after. The drop the stock is in now is left out. Months are trading months (21 trading days). Only today's index members are studied, which flatters the recovery figures.

Options study. Target time = 75th percentile of the stock's past recovery times (1.5 × the median with fewer than 4 past drops, 12 months with none). The expiry is the first listed on or after it. Structures: 100 shares (baseline), long call at the first strike at or above the price, bull call spread selling the strike nearest the prior high, cash-secured put near 95% of the price, and a deep in-the-money LEAPS call (longest expiry 9+ months, strike near 80%). Liquid means a live bid and ask, a spread of 25% of mid or less, and open interest of 100+. The job runs before the market opens, so quotes are the prior session's; recovery odds count from the start of each past drop, not from today; and only today's S&P members are studied, which flatters the odds.

Attention. Reddit: posts in r/wallstreetbets, r/stocks and r/investing naming "$TICKER" or the company, last 7 days vs the weekly average of the 21 days before (needs a Reddit API key set as a repository secret). Google: search interest in "TICKER stock" in the US, last 4 weeks vs the 8 before, through an unofficial route that Google often blocks. Change = the average of the two ratios. The optional boost multiplies a score by 1 + 0.25 × log2(change), limited to between 0.75 and 1.25.
